How Much Does It Cost to Replace a Roof?

The cost to replace a roof varies depending on material, size, and labour requirements.

Average Roof Replacement Costs

  • Basic roofing materials: lower cost
  • Mid-range materials: average cost
  • Premium roofing materials: higher cost

Cost Factors

  • Roof size and slope
  • Type of roofing material
  • Removal of old roofing
  • Labour and access difficulty

How Long Does a Roof Last?

Most roofs last between 15 and 30 years, depending on materials and maintenance.
