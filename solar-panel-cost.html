How Much Do Solar Panels Cost?

The cost of solar panels depends on system size, installation complexity, and location.

Average Solar Panel Costs

  • Small home system: lower cost
  • Medium home system: moderate cost
  • Large home system: higher cost

What Affects the Cost?

  • Number of panels needed
  • Panel quality and brand
  • Roof size and angle
  • Installation labour

Are Solar Panels Worth It?

For many homeowners, solar panels reduce electricity bills over time and add property value.
