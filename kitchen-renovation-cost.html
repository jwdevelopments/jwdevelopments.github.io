How Much Does a Kitchen Renovation Cost?

Kitchen renovation costs depend on size, finishes, and the scope of work.

Average Kitchen Renovation Costs

  • Minor upgrade: lower cost
  • Full renovation: moderate cost
  • Luxury renovation: higher cost

Main Cost Factors

  • Cabinet quality
  • Countertop materials
  • Plumbing and electrical changes
  • Appliances

Is Renovating a Kitchen Worth It?

A well-done kitchen renovation can improve daily use and increase home value.
